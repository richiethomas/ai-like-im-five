I'm doing solopreneur research using Claude Desktop, and I'm having it research 3-4 different ideas at once. I'm trying to keep the mentality of "livestock, not pets" with these ideas, since the goal is to place many small bets and see if any pay off. It's hard when Claude keeps hyping me up saying "yes! that's our assumption proven correct!". I have to make "livestock, not pets" my mantra during moments like that, so I don't get my hopes up only to have them dashed later.
